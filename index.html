About

Hi! I'm Damien Kazan, a simple video editor trying to make my way through the universe (yes I dig Star Wars). I can work on all sorts of projects but I've been told I'm not bad at cutting trailers, so I thought I'd specialize in this!

I also specialize in motion posters, bringing artwork and illustrations to life through animation, something I've been doing for companies for about 10 years now.

All my work is 100% human edited, no generative AI.

If you need me for your project, feel free to contact me below!

Also if you want to have a look at my film & photography work, feel free to check out my other website damienkazan.com
